# War Room — Vision & Goals

**Source of truth for product direction.** When in doubt, prefer this over feature sprawl.

---

## Vision

**War Room is the private clubhouse for friend-group sports pick’em** — starting with college football, built for phones, built to last years, not seasons.

It’s not another ad-soaked fantasy app. It’s the place **your people** go every week: lock picks, talk trash, keep score, and **remember who won what** long after the group chat forgets.

SlateClub is dead. The future is **War Room** — first **FBS / CFB friend leagues**, then the same DNA into NFL and beyond, without losing the soul.

### One line

> **The permanent home for your crew’s Saturdays (and someday Sundays): fair, funny, phone-first, and full of history.**

## What the product *is*

| Pillar | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Private friend leagues** | Invite-only rooms. Your buddies, your code, your lore. |
| **Simple competition** | Confidence picks, Best Bet, prop. No draft, no waivers, no second job. |

## Who it’s for

- **Hosts** who want a league that “just works” and doesn’t need a manual
- **Players** who only open the app on their phone between errands
- **Multi-gen crews** — boomer dad, Gen X uncle, millennial group chat, xennial “tradition” energy
- People who care more about **the room** than DFS or stranger leaderboards

## Experience vision

1. **Join in seconds** — text link → account → you’re in  
2. **Know the job immediately** — “lock this week” or “waiting on the host”  
3. **Lock without stress** — big targets, sticky save, freeze at first kickoff  
4. **Feel the week** — board reveals, standings, locker noise  
5. **End the year loud** — sarcastic finales, engraved hardware, shareable flex  
6. **Come back next August** — defending champ ceremony, same room, deeper history

## Founder ethos (non-negotiables)

- **KISS** — if it needs a tutorial novel, simplify the product  
- **Simple to play, rich to discover** — Layer 1 (join → picks → confidence → Best Bet → Gazette → standings) is a 10/10 alone; depth is endless but never required on day 1. See `docs/DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md`  
- **Fun ≠ clutter** — Disney energy, not 87 rides at the entrance  
- **Longevity** — design for year 5 of the same league, not week 1 churn; veterans *discover* layers that always existed  
- **Fair play** — lock rules, progressive reveal, no shady defaults  
- **Effortless invites** — every member can spread the word, entertaining copy included  
- **Personality without friction** — sarcasm and roast culture *after* the job is clear  
- **Witty shit-talk, never bigotry** — sass and group-chat heat across every sport; never racist, sexist, homophobic, or xenophobic  
- **Host power, player joy** — Commish can run the room; players never feel like admin

## Success looks like

A group of 12–24 friends who:

- Open War Room on their phones every Saturday without thinking  
- Can explain the rules in one text  
- Still argue about last year’s Toilet Bowl in October  
- Invite the next buddy with one tap and a message that actually gets opened  
- Trust the board, the locks, and the hardware

### Product north star (checklist)

- Friend leagues only — private crew, not a public marketplace  
- KISS — one obvious job at a time; effortless on a **phone** first  
- Trust over ads — fair play, clear rules, no grift  
- Saturdays + opinions — confidence, Best Bet, prop; no draft/waivers maze  
- Longevity / living history — trophies, banners, titles, museum, “we remember”  
- Both halves matter — Championship *and* Toilet Bowl; Nerd King for pride  
- Everyone can grow the room — seamless invites, multi-gen share energy  

### What “done” feels like for a user

*“I opened it on my phone, knew exactly what to do in 10 seconds, locked picks, roasted my buddies, and the league still feels special next year.”*
